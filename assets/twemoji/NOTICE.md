# Twemoji

The PNG files in this folder are emoji graphics from **Twemoji**
(<https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji>, tag `v15.1.0`, `assets/72x72`).

Copyright 2019 Twitter, Inc and other contributors.
Copyright 2024 jdecked and other contributors.

The graphics are licensed under **CC-BY 4.0**: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>
They are used here unchanged. The list of included emoji is in `scripts/twemoji_list.py`;
`python scripts/fetch-twemoji.py` downloads them and regenerates `src/twemoji_table.rs`.
